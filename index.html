Good to know

Jorhat cab service FAQs

How far is Kaziranga from Jorhat?

Kaziranga National Park's Kohora gate is about 97 km from Jorhat, roughly 2.5 hours by taxi on NH 715. We recommend leaving Jorhat by 5 am if you want to make the first morning safari.

What is the taxi fare from Jorhat to Kaziranga?

A one way sedan drop from Jorhat to Kaziranga starts at about ₹2,800, and an SUV at about ₹3,500. Round trips and waiting time are quoted separately. WhatsApp us your dates for an exact figure.

How do I book a cab in Jorhat?

Call or WhatsApp +91 99999 00000 with your pickup point, drop point and time. We confirm the vehicle and full fare on the spot. There is no app to install and no advance account needed.

Do you provide Jorhat Airport pickup and drop?

Yes. We cover Rowriah Airport (JRH) around the clock. Share your flight number when booking and we adjust the pickup if your flight runs late.

How do I reach Majuli from Jorhat?

Take a cab from Jorhat to Nimati Ghat, about 14 km away, then a ferry across the Brahmaputra to Kamalabari Ghat on Majuli. We time the drop to the ferry schedule and can arrange your return pickup.

Do you offer one way drops?

Yes. On one way outstation drops you pay only for the outbound route, which usually works out cheaper than a round trip fare when you are not coming back the same day.

Is the cab service available at night?

Yes, we operate 24x7, including late night airport and railway pickups. For departures before 6 am, book the previous evening so we can assign a driver in advance.

Can I book a cab for trips outside Assam?

Yes, we run round trips across the Northeast on request, including Nagaland, Meghalaya and Arunachal Pradesh routes. Tell us your itinerary and we will plan the vehicle and halts.
